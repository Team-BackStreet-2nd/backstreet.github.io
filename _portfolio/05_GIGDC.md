**BackStreet** won the **Silver Award in the University Division** at the 2025 **GIGDC (Global Indie Game Development Competition)**.  

GIGDC is one of Korea’s most prestigious indie game competitions,  
hosted by the Korea Game Developers Association with support from the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and KOCCA.  
Each year, hundreds of entries are submitted, making it a highly competitive and recognized event.  

This award highlights BackStreet’s creativity and quality,  
further solidifying its position as a promising indie game development team.
